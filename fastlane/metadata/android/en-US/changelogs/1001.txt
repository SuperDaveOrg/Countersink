The first release of Countersink.

• Counters in groups, with a history of every count, daily and weekly goals, streaks and charts.
• Counters worked out from others: totals, differences, shares and completed rounds.
• Full-screen counting, volume buttons, a home-screen widget and a quick-settings tile.
• Backups to a file or a folder you choose, and import from other counter apps.
• No ads, no account, no internet permission.
